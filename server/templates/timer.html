Start runs a Timer game: the clock, callouts and reminders, with no box scoring. Callouts and reminders also play during Domination and Rush rounds started from the referee dashboard.

REMINDERS

Repeats count game time, so pausing the clock pauses them. The first plays about a minute into the round, staggered so they don't stack, and they wait for any callout to finish. New reminder phrases are added in server/voice_clips.csv.
